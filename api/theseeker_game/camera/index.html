Module camera 

Source
Expand description

The in-game cameras. The main camera shows the world through the gameplay viewport, and the player overlay camera shares its view to draw the player and the UI above the post-processing. Each frame places them between the framing rig’s last two ticks, shaken.

Framing: where the camera rig heads each tick. A scripted focus or the player’s situation can claim the camera; otherwise it follows the player through the level, holding still while they stay inside a dead zone and leaning toward the open space ahead as they leave it.
