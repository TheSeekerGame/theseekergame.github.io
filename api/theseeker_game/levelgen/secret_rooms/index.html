Module secret_rooms 

Source
Expand description

Secret rooms: rule images carved into the terrain, hidden behind covers until their breakable wall falls.

A rule PNG is centered on the tile it is placed at. Its black, white, #5b666f and #4dfbff pixels are the room, all carved out: white is the breakable wall, #5b666f also gets manual near background and #4dfbff marks ore. Green pixels require terrain; red pixels are the entrance, which must be open cave without spikes.
