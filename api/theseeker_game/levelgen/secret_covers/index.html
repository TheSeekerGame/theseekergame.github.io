Module secret_covers 

Source
Expand description

Secret room covers: the ground as it was before carving, over a room and the border its carving reshades, until the room’s breakable wall falls.

Rooms keep two borders apart (see carve_secret_rooms), so each cover shows exactly what the ground would show there, whichever rooms are open. What grows inside a room stays hidden until it opens: art in its entrance would otherwise show past the cover.
